**Meu Metrônomo** is a free tool that runs entirely in your browser. We do not require sign-up and we do not collect your name, e-mail or any personal data to use the metronome.

## Preferences in the browser

We store in the browser's local storage (localStorage) only usage preferences: your theme choice (light or dark), your language choice, the metronome's last settings (BPM, beats, subdivision, sound) and the presets you save by name. This information does not leave your device and can be deleted by clearing the site's data in your browser.

## Microphone

The [tuner](/afinador/) asks for microphone access to identify the note being played. The audio is analyzed in real time inside your browser and is never recorded, stored or sent to servers. Access ends when you click "Stop" or close the page.

## Ads (Google AdSense)

To keep the site free, we show Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on previous visits to this and other sites. You can turn off ad personalization in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or learn more at [How Google uses information from sites that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Traffic statistics

We may use Google Analytics to measure visits in aggregate (page views, traffic source, device type). This data does not identify you individually.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: write to contato@meumetronomo.com.br.
