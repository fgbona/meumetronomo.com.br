All tools run in your browser, with no sign-up and nothing to install. They work on phones and computers. The metronome and the guides are available in English; the other tools are currently in Portuguese, but their controls are simple enough to use without the language.

## Tempo and timing

- **[Online metronome](/en/)** — BPM from 1 to 240, beats per bar, subdivisions, tap tempo, timer, speed trainer, muted bars, four click sounds and saved settings.
- **[Rhythm trainer](/treino-de-ritmo/)** (pt) — tap along with the click and see the deviation of each tap in milliseconds. Shows whether you drag, rush or wobble, with a silent mode to test your internal time.
- **[Time converter](/conversor-de-tempo/)** (pt) — how many minutes N bars last at a given BPM, and how many bars fit in a duration.
- **[Delay calculator](/calculadora-de-delay/)** (pt) — BPM to milliseconds for each note value (dotted and triplet) and to Hz for LFOs. For synced delay, reverb and compressor.
- **[BPM of a song](/bpm-de-musica/)** (pt) — pick an audio file and find its tempo. Analysis in the browser, no upload.
- **[Polyrhythm metronome](/polirritmia/)** (pt) — two voices, 3 against 2, 4 against 3 or any combination, with separate indicators and the option to mute each voice.
- **[Rhythm reading](/leitura-ritmica/)** (pt) — random exercises with musical figures in four levels, count-in and the option to hear the answer.

## Tuning and ear

- **[Chromatic tuner](/afinador/)** (pt) — tune guitar, bass, ukulele, violin or voice with the microphone, with deviation in cents and adjustable reference A.
- **[Tuning fork and tone generator](/diapasao/)** (pt) — the 12 notes in five octaves, four timbres and a free frequency in Hz.
- **[Ear training](/treino-de-ouvido/)** (pt) — recognize intervals and chords by ear, with three levels and scoring.
- **[Tuning drone](/drone/)** (pt) — continuous pedal note with harmonics and fifth, to practice intonation on strings, winds and voice.

## Harmony

- **[Chord transposer](/transpositor-de-acordes/)** (pt) — paste a chord chart and change the key in semitones. For voice and capo.
- **[Scales and chords](/escalas/)** (pt) — notes, formula and chords of each degree for major, minor, modes, pentatonic, blues and more, in any key.

## Guides

- **[Tempo markings](/en/tempo-markings/)** — table from Largo to Prestissimo and a guide from 40 to 200 BPM.
- **[BPM by genre](/bpm-por-estilo/)** (pt) — typical tempo of samba, forró, funk, rock, house, jazz and dozens of other styles.
- **[How to practice with a metronome](/en/how-to-practice-with-a-metronome/)** — BPM progression method and a 15-minute routine.
