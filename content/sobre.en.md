**Meu Metrônomo** ("My Metronome" in Portuguese) is a free online metronome made to be opened and used in seconds: no sign-up, no install, no ads covering the screen. It works in any modern browser, on a computer or a phone. The site is Brazilian and most of its tools are in Portuguese; the metronome and the practice guides are also available in English.

## Why it exists

Anyone learning an instrument needs a metronome every day. Phone apps usually ask for installation, permissions and a subscription to unlock basic features. Many website metronomes are slow, full of ads or have imprecise clicks. Meu Metrônomo was born to fix that with a light page that loads fast, keeps time precisely and has only what a musician really uses: BPM, beats per bar, accent, subdivisions, tap tempo, timer, speed trainer and muted bars. Over time the site gained other [tools](/en/tools/) a musician needs in the same session: tuner, tuning fork, delay calculator, bar-to-minutes converter, rhythm trainer and ear training.

## How it works

The metronome runs entirely in your browser. The clicks are generated by the **Web Audio API** and scheduled on the browser's own audio clock, which is far more precise than ordinary JavaScript timers. That is why the pulse does not drift even when the page stays open for a long time. No data leaves your device: the only information stored, in the browser's local storage, is your theme preference, your language choice and the metronome settings and presets. The tuner analyzes the microphone sound on the device itself, without recording or sending anything.

The site is generated with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the source code is open, available at [github.com/fgbona/meumetronomo.com.br](https://github.com/fgbona/meumetronomo.com.br). Suggestions, corrections and bug reports are welcome there.

## The articles

Besides the tool, the site has practical articles about practicing with a metronome, written for guitarists, drummers, pianists and players of any other instrument. The goal is to answer the questions that come up in the practice room: why start slowly, how to raise the tempo, what subdivisions are, which BPM to use for each style. Start with [How to practice with a metronome](/en/how-to-practice-with-a-metronome/) or the [tempo markings guide](/en/tempo-markings/).

## How the site is supported

Meu Metrônomo is an independent project maintained in spare time. To cover server and domain costs, the site shows Google AdSense ads in positions that do not get in the way of the metronome. See the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy/) to learn how ads and traffic statistics work.
